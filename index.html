Experience fire-grilled cuts, warm service and a room made for good company at The Butcher’s Block — the premium steakhouse in the heart of Hulhumalé. Find us on Nirolhu Magu, next to Dhiraagu Tower, whether you’re meeting friends, celebrating with family, or sitting down to a proper steak dinner.

Meat, mastered

From Brazilian tenderloin and ribeye to our Wagyu T-bone signature cut, every steak is fire-grilled to order and rested properly before it reaches your table. Add slow-braised lamb shank, flame-grilled bone marrow and fresh seafood, and you have a steakhouse built for meat lovers.
