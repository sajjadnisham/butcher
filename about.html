Welcome

The Butcher’s Block is a premium steakhouse in Hulhumalé, Maldives, dedicated to one thing above all: great meat, cooked with care. You’ll find us at Lot 10035, Nirolhu Magu, Goalhi 1, next to Dhiraagu Tower — step inside for warm lighting, a relaxed room and the smell of the grill.

We specialize in high-quality, carefully sourced cuts of beef, prepared to highlight their natural tenderness. Our Brazilian steaks — tenderloin, boneless ribeye and New York cut sirloin — share the menu with slow-smoked US beef short plate, a thick-carved prime rib and our Wagyu T-bone signature cut.

Every steak is fire-grilled to order and rested properly, then served with house sides and sauces.

Our team is at your service for everything from a quiet dinner for two to a celebration for the whole family.
