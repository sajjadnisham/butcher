About

Our Story

A butcher's respect for meat.

The Butcher's Block

Good meat, treated well.

The Butcher's Block started with a simple idea: bring an honest steakhouse to Hulhumalé. A place where the meat is the star, where every cut is handled with a butcher's care, and where you can sit down for a great steak any day of the week.

We're on Nirolhu Goalhi, open for lunch and dinner every day. Come as you are.

The craft

Every steak, start to finish.

We choose our cuts carefully, portion them by weight and cook them over serious heat. Then we let them rest, because a steak that's rested properly is a steak that stays juicy on the plate.

Tell us how you like it and we'll get it there, from rare to well done.
