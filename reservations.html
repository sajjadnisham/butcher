Good to know
How do I reserve?

Fill in the form and we'll write a WhatsApp message with your details. Send it to us and we'll confirm your table. You can also call +960 989-9981.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome whenever we have space. Booking ahead is the best way to guarantee a table, especially on weekends.

Can you host large groups?

Yes. For bigger groups and celebrations see our private events page.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Let us know about allergies or dietary needs in your booking note and we'll do our best to look after you. For questions about halal status or specific ingredients, please ask us directly.
